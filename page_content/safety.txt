SECTION 4: SAFETY PROTOCOLS ACCORDION (resources/js/components/home-safety-practices-section.tsx)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Section Label:
Safety Protocols

Heading:
Disciplined Field Standards

Intro Copy:
Every mobile crane deployment follows six non-negotiable operational safeguards, from initial ground bearing verification and statutory load testing to on-site exclusion zones and unconditional Stop Work Authority.

Accordion Protocols:
Protocol 01: Engineered Lift Planning & Ground Verification
- Every lift begins with comprehensive technical evaluation before wheels roll or outriggers extend. Our lift planners assess site access corridors, ground bearing capacity, outrigger pad displacement, and safe working radiuses.
- For complex, oversized, or tandem crane lifts, we produce CAD-engineered 2D/3D lift studies, rigging diagrams, and risk mitigation steps to eliminate variables and surprises on site.

Protocol 02: Statutory Load Testing & Certified Rigging Tackle (DMR 18)
- Every mobile crane in our fleet is backed by current LME and LMI statutory inspection records in full compliance with Driven Machinery Regulations (DMR 18).
- Slings, shackles, spreader beams, and auxiliary rigging tackle undergo rigorous quarterly colour-coded tracking, certified proof-load testing, and mandatory daily pre-use checklists.

Protocol 03: TETA-Accredited Operators & Qualified Master Riggers
- Precision machinery requires verified expertise in the cab and on the ground. Our crane operators, master riggers, and lift directors hold full TETA accreditations and valid Annexure 3 medical fitness certificates.
- Ongoing competency assessments and defensive operational training ensure dependable execution across every industry and site environment, whether on a short-term crane rental or a long-term project deployment.

Protocol 04: Project-Specific RAMS & Daily Toolbox Briefings
- Prior to mobilisation, we issue tailored Risk Assessments and Method Statements (RAMS) structured around your project’s exact site constraints and operational envelope.
- Daily on-site toolbox sessions establish radio frequencies, banksman hand signals, blind-spot awareness, and barricaded exclusion zones before outriggers are deployed.

Protocol 05: Uncompromising Stop Work Authority
- Safety takes precedence over deadlines without exception. Every Target Cranes crew member, driver, and site supervisor holds unconditional Stop Work Authority.
- If sudden wind gusts exceed manufacturer charts, ground shifts occur, or unexpected site hazards arise, operations immediately pause until safe conditions are verified.

Protocol 06: OHSA 85 of 1993 & National SANS Standard Compliance
- Our Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) management protocols align strictly with the South African Occupational Health and Safety Act, Construction Regulations 2014, and applicable SANS lifting codes.
- We arrive on site with complete, audit-ready safety documentation to streamline principal contractor inductions and client audits.

Compliance & Regulations

Strict Adherence To National Standards

Body Copy:
Our health and safety management system is designed to integrate seamlessly with principal contractor safety policies across every industry and sector in South Africa. We support both short-term crane rentals and long-term project deployments.

Compliance Cards:
1. Occupational Health and Safety Act 85 of 1993
   Full compliance with South African OHSA statutory mandates, Section 8 general duties, and Construction Regulations 2014.

2. Driven Machinery Regulations (DMR 18)
   Regular calibration of safe load indicators (SLI / LMI), boom angle sensors, and anti-two-block safety limit switches.

3. Rigging Tackle & Lifting Hardware Inspection
   Colour-coded quarterly inspection tags on all steel wire ropes, web slings, shackles, chain blocks, and spreader beams.
