Company Overview

Built On Experience, Trusted Across South Africa

Body Copy:
Headquartered in Bellville South, Cape Town, Target Cranes provides flexible short- and long-term mobile crane hire, heavy rigging, and managed lifting operations across all industries in South Africa.

We are never limited to a single sector. From quick once-off equipment lifts and short-term crane rentals to long-term contract deployments and complex multi-crane operations, our work is defined by rigorous preparation, verified equipment reliability, and close collaboration with on-site teams.
